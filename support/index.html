Frequently asked

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. GYMeter works entirely on iPhone. The Apple Watch app is optional — if you have one, it runs the same session on your wrist and stays in sync with the phone.

Does it work with the screen locked, or the phone in a bag?

Yes — that's what it's built for. GYMeter plays audible countdown cues at the end of every rest period, and shows a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island so you can check the timer without unlocking.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. GYMeter works completely offline and makes no network requests at all. Gyms are exactly where signal is worst, so nothing in the app depends on having any.

Are my workouts saved to Apple Health?

Yes, if you grant permission. Completed workouts are written to Health, and on Apple Watch the app also reads heart rate and active energy during the session. You can change this any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

The set timer keeps counting past the expected time. Is that a bug?

No — that's deliberate. The set standard is a reference, not a cap: it's what progress is measured against, not something that cuts your set short. Nothing ends a set except you.

How do I back up my routines?

GYMeter can export all your routines and workout history to a single file that you save wherever you like — Files, iCloud Drive, anywhere. You can import it back on the same device or a new one. The export never leaves your control; the app doesn't upload it.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no data collection of any kind.
