GYMeter Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

GYMeter ("the app") is a strength-training interval timer for iPhone and Apple Watch, developed by German Marquez. This policy explains what data the app touches — and, more to the point, what it doesn't do with it.

Short version

GYMeter collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers — the app makes no network connections at all. Everything it touches stays on your own device, or in a file you export yourself.

Health data

GYMeter integrates with Apple Health (HealthKit) to make your training log more useful:

  • On Apple Watch, the app reads your heart rate and active energy during a workout session, and writes the completed workout to Health.
  • On iPhone, the app writes completed workouts to Health.

All of this happens directly on your device through Apple's HealthKit framework. GYMeter never transmits Health data anywhere — there is no server for it to go to.

Your routines and workout history

Your routines, exercises, and session history are stored locally on your device. If you use the app's backup feature, GYMeter can export that data to a file you choose and save wherever you like (Files, iCloud Drive, etc.) — that export is entirely under your control and is never uploaded by the app itself.

iPhone ↔ Apple Watch sync

If you use GYMeter on both iPhone and Apple Watch, the two devices exchange routines and session state directly over Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This stays between your own paired devices and never leaves them.

What GYMeter does not do

  • No account creation, no sign-in.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising or ad tracking.
  • No network requests of any kind — the app works entirely offline.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, because nothing is collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

GYMeter is not directed at children and does not collect information from anyone, of any age — see above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it.
